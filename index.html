Before you start: how to be a great secret shopper

  1. Know what to look for. Tap the button below and read the questions before you order. Your answers save on this phone, so you can fill them in after your visit.
  2. Order in person in the drive-thru only. Order with an outside order taker in the drive-thru. Do not order in the Chick-fil-A app, and do not order inside for dine-in or carry-out.
  3. Act like a normal guest. Do not tell the team that you are a secret shopper.
  4. Get the name. Read the name tag of the team member who takes your order. You need it to submit.
  5. Scan your app with your order taker. Scan your Chick-fil-A One app when they ask, or before you pay, so we know where to send your reward.
  6. Keep your receipt. You will enter the transaction number from it.
  7. Fill it out as soon as you can. Best: start while you wait in line. Your receipt with the transaction number comes with your order. Add a note for anything that was missed.
  8. Submit when you are done. Tap Submit to store 2 times. After that, the evaluation can’t be changed.
